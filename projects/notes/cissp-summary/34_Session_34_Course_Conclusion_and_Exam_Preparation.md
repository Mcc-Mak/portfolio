## III. Key Themes Across All Domains

### Security Principles (Domain 1)
- Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability (CIA triad)
- Least privilege, need-to-know, separation of duties
- Defense in depth

### Technical Controls (Domains 3, 4, 7, 8)
- Firewalls (packet filtering, stateful, NGFW)
- IDS/IPS (network-based, host-based, signature/behavior)
- Cryptography (symmetric, asymmetric, hashing)
- Network segmentation (physical, logical, micro-segmentation)

### Identity and Access (Domain 5)
- Authentication factors (Type 1, 2, 3)
- SSO, Kerberos, SAML, OAuth, OIDC
- Access control models (DAC, MAC, RBAC, ABAC)

### Testing and Assessment (Domain 6)
- Vulnerability assessments vs. penetration testing
- SAST vs. DAST
- SOC reports (1, 2, 3), PCI DSS levels, CSA STAR

### Operations and Resilience (Domains 1, 7)
- BCP vs. DRP
- MTD, RTO, RPO
- Recovery sites (hot, warm, cold)
- Incident response lifecycle

### Software Security (Domain 8)
- SDLC phases (initiation → development → implementation → O&M → disposal)
- Agile (4 core values, Scrum)
- DevSecOps (CI/CD, security as code)
- OWASP Top 10 (2017 and 2021)

## VI. Final Exam Tips (Review from Early Course Lesson)

1. **Think like a manager** – CISSP exams test managerial decision-making, not just technical knowledge
2. **Read questions carefully** – Look for keywords: "BEST," "MOST," "LEAST," "FIRST," "PRIMARY"
3. **Eliminate wrong answers** – Narrow down to two options, then choose the more secure/managerial answer
4. **Look for the end goal** – Many questions ask "what is the PRIMARY purpose?" or "what should be done FIRST?"
5. **Remember human life is top priority** – Safety overrides all other concerns
6. **Know your frameworks** – NIST, ISO, COBIT, ITIL, OWASP
7. **Understand BCP/DRP concepts** – MTD, RTO, RPO, recovery sites, testing types
8. **Know access control models** – DAC, MAC, RBAC, ABAC
9. **Understand SDLC and development methodologies** – Waterfall, Agile, Scrum, DevOps, DevSecOps
10. **Manage your time** – 3 hours for up to 150 questions (~1.2 minutes per question)
