# Domain 2 - Asset Security - Answer Keys

## Question 58 (2.1.1)
**Correct Answer:** A. Information classification

**Feedback:** Information classification addresses the impact or loss to the organization if the confidentiality, availability, integrity, authenticity, or non-repudiation of their information is compromised, either by an insider or someone outside of the organization.

## Question 59 (2.1.2)
**Correct Answer:** A. Marking is the external identification of assets, while labeling involves embedding security attributes in internal data structures.

**Feedback:** Marking involves placing human-readable information on assets, while labeling embeds security attributes internally. This ensures easy identification and communication of security levels and classifications.

## Question 62 (2.1, 2.3.2)
**Correct Answer:** B. Brand reputation

**Feedback:** Brand reputation is an intangible asset. It represents the value derived from the perception and standing of a company's brand in the marketplace.

## Question 63 (2.3.2)
**Correct Answer:** C. Software

**Feedback:** Software, however, is not considered tangible in a traditional sense; although it does have a physical presence, it cannot be touched even though it can be copied many times. Under accounting rules, businesses can capitalize software as if it were tangible.

## Question 66 (2.4.1)
**Correct Answer:** D. The data steward

**Feedback:** The data steward is responsible for the data content, context, and the associated business rules.

## Question 67 (2.4.1)
**Correct Answer:** D. Accountability for protecting data while in custody

**Feedback:** A data processor processes data on behalf of the data controller, and while they may be given the responsibility of protecting the data, accountability remains with the controller.

## Question 70 (2.4.5, 2.6.3)
**Correct Answer:** C. Documenting a records schedule that specifies the retention period for each type of information and ensuring all departments adhere to this schedule without exceeding these periods

**Feedback:** Documenting a records schedule that specifies the retention requirements for each type of information and ensuring these guidelines are followed is the best practice. This approach ensures compliance with relevant laws and regulations and helps manage data efficiently, retaining only what is necessary and for the appropriate duration.

## Question 82 (2.6.4)
**Correct Answer:** D. End-to-end

**Feedback:** In end-to-end encryption, the data is encrypted at the start of the transmission by the sender and is decrypted by the receiver.

## Question 83 (2.6.4)
**Correct Answer:** C. Link encryption encrypts both the data and the routing information in the packet, requiring decryption and re-encryption at each node in the communication path, potentially exposing data at these points.

**Feedback:** Link encryption encrypts all data within a packet, including the routing information. At each node along the communication path, such as satellites or routers, the packet is decrypted and then re-encrypted. This process ensures traffic confidentiality but also potentially exposes data at these intermediate points if a node is compromised.
