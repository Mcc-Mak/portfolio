# Domain 6 - Security Assessment and Testing - Answer Keys

## Question 170 (6.1.1)
**Correct Answer:** C. Implementing and reviewing detailed logs of all system processing activities

**Feedback:** The "Processing Integrity" principle in SOC2 focuses on the completeness, validity, accuracy, timeliness, and authorization of system processing. Implementing and regularly reviewing detailed logs of all system processing activities is crucial for ensuring the processing is accurate, authorized, and functioning as intended, thereby meeting the requirements of the processing integrity principle.

## Question 173 (6.1.1-3)
**Correct Answer:** C. SOC 1 Type II

**Feedback:** A SOC 1 Type II report would be appropriate since it would reflect the effectiveness of the internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR). Special attention could be associated with benefits management. SOC 1 is for reviewing financial controls. SOC 1 Type II proves design effectiveness of financial control.

## Question 178 (6.1.3)
**Correct Answer:** B. Authentication

**Feedback:** The five criteria are Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Privacy, and Process integrity. Authentication is not a part of the SOC 2 audit.

## Question 179 (6.2.2)
**Correct Answer:** B. A team of ethical hackers is hired to simulate an attack on the institution's network to identify and exploit potential security weaknesses.

**Feedback:** Penetration testing, unlike a vulnerability assessment, involves actively attempting to exploit vulnerabilities in a system to understand the real-world effectiveness of existing security measures.

## Question 182 (6.2.10)
**Correct Answer:** A. Substantive

**Feedback:** A substantive test evaluates the proper operation of the process whereas compliance tests determine if, in the opinion of the controls assessor, the control exists and is operating properly.
