## V3 - Exam Tips

### 1. Exam Mindset & Question Approach
- **No trick questions** – questions are precise and match study material.
- **Read questions multiple times** to ensure understanding.
- **Identify distractors/red herrings** – at least one wrong answer is designed to mislead; eliminating it improves odds.
- **Pay attention to bold, italics, or uppercase words** – these signal key terms the testers emphasize.

### 2. Answer Selection Strategy
- **Use CompTIA/book knowledge, not personal workplace experience** – workplace terminology may differ.
- **Select the “book answer”** – test writers follow official materials.
- **Choose the “best answer”** – several may be correct, but pick the one true in most situations.
- **Don’t fight the question** – identify the key concept the writer is testing.

### 3. Keyword Associations (Examples Given)
- **Confidentiality** → Encryption
- **Integrity** → Hashing
- **Availability** → Redundancy & Resiliency

### 4. Memorization & Question Format
- **No need to memorize word-for-word** – only recognize terms from answer choices.
- **No fill-in-the-blank** – questions are multiple choice, multiple selection, or PBQs.
- **Vendor neutral / generic** – no specific software syntax or device commands required.
- **Tool knowledge** – know *what* a tool is used for (e.g., Nmap = network mapping), not exact command syntax.

### 5. Study Planning & Certification Timeline
- **Set a target date** – 30, 60, or 90 days (not longer than 90 days).
- **Work backwards** from target date to plan daily lessons.
- **Total study time needed**: ~40 hours.
- **Study plans available** (PDFs for 30‑day and 60‑day plans from Lesson 2).
